4Analyse & stitch maps

Joins the photos into one map of the field, then checks it for crop health and weeds.

Analyse photos & stitch maps
3D matching or GPS placement?

3D matching (structure from motion, with OpenDroneMap) finds the same plants in overlapping photos, works out exactly where the camera was and rebuilds the field's surface, then makes one seamless, true-to-scale map. Best for photos straight off the drone. Needs Docker Desktop, about 10 GB free disk, photos that overlap (scans planned here do) and several minutes.

GPS placement puts each photo where the drone's GPS says it was. Takes about 1 second per photo, but seams can be off by a few metres.

6Field health summary

Show where to look
Show weed map
How these numbers work

Crop health is the share of the field that looks healthy. Each photo is split into areas of about 4 m × 4 m. An area needs a look when its crop is thin (green cover under 70% of the field's typical cover), pale (clearly less green than the field's typical plants) or has bare or stressed patches. 85–100 is Good, 65–84 Fair, below 65 Poor.

Weeds is the share of the photographed field covered by likely weed patches, found by the weed model. Under 2% is Low, 2–10% Moderate, over 10% High.

Crop health compares each part of the field with the rest of the same field, so it can't tell if the whole field is evenly stressed. Compare scans over time for that.
